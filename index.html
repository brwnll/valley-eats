About

A guide to eating in the upper Snoqualmie Valley: every restaurant, café, pub, and food truck in North Bend and Snoqualmie, filtered to what's open on the day you look. Times are Pacific.

About the data

Hours and menus change; each listing links to the restaurant's own site or map listing, so check there before you drive over. "Popular dishes" come from menus, review sites, and local coverage, so they're a best guess rather than a ranking. Order is by Google and Yelp review count when the list was compiled.

Data last updated . Spotted a mistake? Edit data.js in the repository.

Photos

Restaurant photos come from Google Maps and are credited to their photographers. Dish photos are representative pictures of each kind of dish from Wikimedia Commons, not photos taken at these restaurants.

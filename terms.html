Terms of use

Restaurant photos and ratings come from Google Maps Platform. By using this site you agree that your use of that content is also governed by the Google Terms of Service. Dish photos are openly licensed images from Wikimedia Commons; see the photo credits.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, no forms, and no analytics, and it stores nothing about you. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and restaurant photos load from Google, so Google receives the requests your browser makes for them under the Google Privacy Policy.
